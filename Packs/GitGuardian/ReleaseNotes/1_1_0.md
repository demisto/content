
#### Integrations

##### New: GitGuardian SaaS

New: Added the **GitGuardian SaaS** integration, which collects secret incidents and audit logs from the GitGuardian SaaS platform (v1.1 API) into the `gitguardian_saas_raw` dataset. <~XSIAM> (Available from Cortex XSIAM 2.11).</~XSIAM>

##### GitGuardian Enterprise

Renamed the integration display name from *GitGuardian Event Collector* to *GitGuardian Enterprise* to distinguish it from the new GitGuardian SaaS integration.

#### Modeling Rules

##### New: GitGuardian SaaS Modeling Rule

New: Added a modeling rule that maps GitGuardian SaaS secret incidents and audit logs to XDM. <~XSIAM> (Available from Cortex XSIAM 2.11).</~XSIAM>
