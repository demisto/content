
#### Scripts

##### MicrosoftGraphFilesApiModule

- Added the ***get-file-content*** command to retrieve the content of a single SharePoint/OneDrive file from a sharing URL. Supported formats: ``.md``, ``.json``, ``.jsonl``, ``.csv`` and ``.docx`` (up to 5 MB). Text formats are returned inline, binary formats are returned Base64-encoded.
