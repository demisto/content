
#### Integrations

##### Strata Logging Service XSOAR Connector

- Fixed an issue where fetching incidents failed when the *Fetch Filter* parameter was used together with the *Severity of events to fetch (Firewall)* or *Subtype of events to fetch (Firewall)* parameters set to **all**.
- Updated the integration documentation and the *Fetch Filter* parameter help text to clarify that *Fetch Filter* cannot be used with specific *Severity of events to fetch (Firewall)* or *Subtype of events to fetch (Firewall)* values.
