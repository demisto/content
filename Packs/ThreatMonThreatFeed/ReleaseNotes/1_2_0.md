#### Integrations

##### ThreatMon Threat Feed

- Added the ThreatMon IOC details that the ThreatMon TAXII server now publishes to the indicators:
  - The ThreatMon score in the new **ThreatMon Score** field and the ThreatMon status (Active or Inactive) in the new **ThreatMon Status** field.
  - The severity in **Source Original Severity**, the confidence level in **Confidence**, the reporting sources in **Reported By** and the categories in **Category**.
  - The country in **Geo Country** and the ISP in **AS Owner**, for IP and URL indicators.
  - The time ThreatMon last saw the IOC in **Last Seen By Source**.
  - The score, severity, confidence level, status, categories, ISP, country and resolved IP addresses in the indicator description.
- Added the *Set Reputation by ThreatMon Severity* parameter. When selected, Critical and High indicators get the Bad reputation and Medium and Low indicators get the Suspicious reputation.
- Indicators are sent again when ThreatMon changes their score, severity or status.
- Fixed an issue where the ***threatmon-get-indicators*** command returned no indicators when the first page of the collection was empty.
- Note: Indicators fetched before this version get the new details when ThreatMon publishes them again. To add the details to them now, run the ***threatmon-reset-fetch-state*** command. The next fetch reads the collections again from the *First Fetch Time* and updates the existing indicators.

#### Indicator Fields

##### ThreatMon Score

- New: The ThreatMon risk score of the indicator, from 0 to 100.

##### ThreatMon Status

- New: The ThreatMon status of the indicator (Active or Inactive).
