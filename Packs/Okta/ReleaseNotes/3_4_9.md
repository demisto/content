##### Okta 2.0 Modeling Rule

- Updated the *target* user selection to resolve the *xdm.target.user.upn*, *xdm.target.user.username*, and related user fields from the *target* element of type *User* for the *okta_okta_raw* and *okta_sso_raw* datasets.
