#### Integrations

##### Feedly Feed

- CVE indicators now include the CVSS score, version, vector and table, the vulnerable products (CPE), and their CWE ids as tags.
- Added support for targeted organizations (Identity), countries (Location) and affected products (Software), with their relationships.
- Fixed an issue where *originates-from* and *located-at* relationships were dropped.
- Indicators related to a tool are now tagged with the tool's name, as for malware and threat actors.
