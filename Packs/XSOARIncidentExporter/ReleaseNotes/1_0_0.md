
#### Integrations

##### XSOAR Incident Exporter - Alert Pusher

- Push XSOAR 6 or XSOAR 8 incidents to Cortex XSIAM as parsed alerts via the Insert Parsed Alerts API.
- Incremental sync support with automatic state tracking.
- Full incident context including labels, custom fields, and raw JSON.

#### Scripts

##### CloseFromXSOAR

- Automatically close XSIAM cases when the source XSOAR incident is closed.

##### SyncXSOARCloseStatus

- Scheduled job script to bulk-sync close status from XSOAR-sourced alerts.

#### Playbooks

##### Close XSIAM Case From XSOAR

- Trigger playbook for XSOAR-sourced alerts to extract and apply close status.
