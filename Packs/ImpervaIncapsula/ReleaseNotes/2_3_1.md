#### Integrations

##### Imperva Incapsula Event Collector v2

- **3x Backlog Recovery Throughput**: Extended the single-cycle batch execution timeout budget from 50s to 110s (`FETCH_TIMEOUT_SAFETY_SECONDS = 110`), allowing the collector to ingest up to 500 files (~325,000+ events) in a single polling run.
- **Enhanced Connection Pool Capacity**: Scaled HTTP Keep-Alive session connection pooling to `max(32, max_workers * 2)` to eliminate connection queueing and latency overhead during high-concurrency worker execution.
- **Optimized Default Max Logs**: Increased `DEFAULT_MAX_LOGS` from 100 to 500 to seamlessly absorb large traffic spikes without falling behind.
