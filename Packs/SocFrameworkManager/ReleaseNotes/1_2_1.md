
#### Integrations

##### SOC Framework Pack Manager

- Fixed **socfw-install-pack** defaulting to the direct ZIP upload, which installs a pack's version record without its content — it registers the pack on the tenant and installs none of its scripts, lists, playbooks or fields. Verified against a live tenant: the same pack ZIP through the direct path set the version and left all 24 of its scripts and lists missing, while the demisto-sdk path installed all 24. The **use_sdk** default is now **true**. Setting it false is a deliberate choice to register a pack without its content, not a faster install.
