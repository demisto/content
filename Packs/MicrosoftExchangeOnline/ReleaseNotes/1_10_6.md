
#### Integrations

##### EWS O365

- Improved reliability of incident fetching so that temporary issues on the mail server (such as the server being busy or briefly unavailable) no longer cause the fetch to fail. Fetching now recovers automatically on the next cycle.
- Fixed an issue where fetching incidents could intermittently fail when a fetched email was opened or changed at the same time it was being marked as read. Such emails are now handled gracefully and no longer interrupt the fetch.
