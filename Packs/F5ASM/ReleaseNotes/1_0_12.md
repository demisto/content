
#### Modeling Rules

##### F5 ASM

- Limited the F5 ASM modeling rule to Cortex XSIAM 2.10 and below. Mappings for Cortex XSIAM 2.11 and later are now handled in a new version of the modeling rule.

##### New: F5 ASM Modeling Rule
