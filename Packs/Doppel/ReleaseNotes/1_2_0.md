#### Integrations

##### Doppel

- Added the *last_activity_timestamp* and *page_size* arguments to the ***doppel-get-alerts*** command, enabling retrieval of alerts filtered by their last activity time.
- Added reopen support for revived alerts. When Doppel moves an alert back into an active queue (for example, a taken-down domain that comes back online), incoming mirroring now reopens the corresponding closed incident instead of silently updating its fields.
- Added the *x-doppel-client* attribution header to every Doppel API request. The header identifies the integration and pack version for usage attribution on Doppel's side and does not affect request handling.

#### Scripts

##### New: DoppelSyncAlerts

- New: Polls Doppel for alerts whose state changed since the last sweep and applies the changes (queue state, entity state, severity, notes, and audit logs) to the matching incidents/issues. When Doppel revives an alert into an active queue, the matching closed incident or issue is reopened (controlled by the *reopen_revived* argument, enabled by default). Intended for platforms without native mirroring, such as Cortex XSIAM.

##### New: DoppelPushUpdates

- New: Archives Doppel alerts whose incidents/issues were closed by analysts, optionally sending the close notes as the alert comment. Incidents closed with the **Duplicate** close reason are skipped. Intended for platforms without native mirroring, such as Cortex XSIAM.

#### Playbooks

##### New: Doppel - Sync Alerts

- New: Runs the DoppelSyncAlerts and DoppelPushUpdates scripts in sequence to keep Doppel incidents/issues and the Doppel platform in sync on deployments without native mirroring.

#### Jobs

##### New: Doppel - Sync Alerts

- New: A scheduled job (every 15 minutes by default) that triggers the **Doppel - Sync Alerts** playbook. On Cortex XSOAR, prefer the integration's native mirroring and leave this job disabled.
