
#### Integrations

##### Palo Alto Networks PAN-OS

- Added the *timeout_in_seconds* (default: *3600*) and *interval_in_seconds* (default: *30*) polling arguments to the ***pan-os-download-latest-content-update***, ***pan-os-download-latest-antivirus-update***, ***pan-os-download-latest-wildfire-update***, ***pan-os-download-latest-gp-update***, ***pan-os-install-latest-content-update***, ***pan-os-install-latest-antivirus-update***, ***pan-os-install-latest-wildfire-update***, and ***pan-os-install-latest-gp-update*** commands, replacing the previous hardcoded polling timeout of *300* seconds and interval of *10* seconds.
- Added the *polling* argument to the ***pan-os-download-latest-antivirus-update***, ***pan-os-download-latest-wildfire-update***, ***pan-os-download-latest-gp-update***, ***pan-os-install-latest-antivirus-update***, ***pan-os-install-latest-wildfire-update***, and ***pan-os-install-latest-gp-update*** commands.
