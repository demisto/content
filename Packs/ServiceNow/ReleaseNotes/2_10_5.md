
#### Modeling Rules

##### New: ServiceNow Modeling Rule

- New: Added a new modeling rule version that maps the `xdm.*.identity.*` fields alongside the existing `xdm.*.user.*` fields.
<~XSIAM> (Available from Cortex XSIAM 2.11).</~XSIAM>

##### ServiceNow ServiceNow Modeling Rule

- Limited the modeling rule to Cortex XSIAM 2.10 and below, and moved the XDM 2.11 field mappings to the new modeling rule version.
