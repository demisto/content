
#### Incident Types

##### CertManagerSaaS Certificate Expiry

- Now runs the **CertManagerSaaS Certificate Expiry Remediation** playbook automatically.

##### CertManagerSaaS Revocation Failed

- Now runs the **CertManagerSaaS Certificate Compromise Response** playbook automatically.

#### Playbooks

##### New: CertManagerSaaS Certificate Compliance Scan

- New: Finds self-signed, weak-key, deprecated-hash and over-long-validity certificates in one inventory pass.

##### New: CertManagerSaaS Certificate Compromise Response

- New: Handles a compromised certificate: enriches it, shows where it is installed, asks an analyst to confirm, revokes it and requests a replacement. Also retries a failed revocation.

##### New: CertManagerSaaS Certificate Expiry Remediation

- New: Runs on every expiry issue: refreshes the certificate, fills the issue fields, sets the severity, and assigns the case to the certificate owner or requests a replacement.

##### New: CertManagerSaaS Certificate Renewal and Keystore Download

- New: Example playbook that renews a certificate and downloads it with its private key and chain. It stops before installing the certificate on a target.

##### New: CertManagerSaaS Certificate Request

- New: Reusable sub-playbook that requests a certificate, waits until it is issued and returns the outcome. It can also download the private key and chain.

#### Triggers Recommendations

##### New: CertManagerSaaS Certificate Expiry Trigger

- New: Runs the **CertManagerSaaS Certificate Expiry Remediation** playbook on expiry issues.

##### New: CertManagerSaaS Revocation Failed Trigger

- New: Runs the **CertManagerSaaS Certificate Compromise Response** playbook on revocation-failed issues.
