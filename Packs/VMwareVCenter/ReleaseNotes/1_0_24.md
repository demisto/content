
#### Modeling Rules

##### New: VMware Vcenter 2.11 Modeling Rule

- Added a new **VMware Vcenter 2.11 Modeling Rule** (available from XSIAM 8.15.0) that maps the `xdm.<source|intermediate|target>.identity.*` fields in addition to the existing `xdm.<source|intermediate|target>.user.*` fields. The `user.*` mappings are unchanged, so existing queries and dashboards continue to work.
- The **VMware Vcenter 2.9 Modeling Rule** now applies up to XSIAM 8.14.0.
