
#### Integrations

##### New: ViewDNS

- New: Added the **ViewDNS** integration, which enriches and pivots on domains, IP addresses, and registrants with ViewDNS.info DNS, IP, and WHOIS intelligence.

#### Playbooks

##### New: ViewDNS - Domain Enrichment

- New: Added the **ViewDNS - Domain Enrichment** playbook, which retrieves the WHOIS record, IP history, and DNS records of a domain.

##### New: ViewDNS - Infrastructure Pivot

- New: Added the **ViewDNS - Infrastructure Pivot** playbook, which lists the domains co-hosted with an IP address and the domains registered with a registrant email address.
