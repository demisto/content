# Qualys ETM

Qualys Enterprise TruRisk Management (ETM) is Qualys' unified platform for risk management based on TruRisk. It consolidates findings relating to vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, malware, and compliance from multiple sources (Qualys, Wiz, Microsoft, Nessus, CrowdStrike Spotlight, and others) into a unified view with a risk score (TruRisk Score).

## What does this pack do?

This pack enables you to manage the lifecycle of Qualys ETM finding reports directly from Cortex:

- List the active reports in your subscription.
- Retrieve the full details of a specific report, including its downloadable resources.
- Download an individual report resource (part-file) to the War Room.
- Generate a new findings report asynchronously, filtered by asset and finding QQL (Qualys Query Language) queries, and automatically download it when it completes.
- Delete one or more reports.
